Each piece in our Artisan Ceramic Collection is hand-thrown on a potter's wheel by skilled craftspeople in our Portuguese studio. The reactive glaze creates unique variations — no two pieces are exactly alike.

Inspired by organic forms found in nature, the gentle undulations and earthy tones bring a sense of calm and authenticity to any interior space. Perfect as a standalone sculptural piece or arranged with dried botanicals.

"Every imperfection tells the story of the maker's hand."
